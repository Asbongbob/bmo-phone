Voice

Drop MP3s in a voice/ folder in your repo — hello.mp3, yay.mp3, ok.mp3, done.mp3, bye.mp3, boot.mp3 — and he'll use them for those moments instead of the phone voice.

Camera

Press and hold his face to take a photo and ask him what he can see.

Live voice

In Live mode the red button opens a real conversation — he hears you continuously, answers in his own voice, and you can cut him off mid-sentence. Sessions last about 10 minutes, then he reconnects on his own.

Listening

With this on he listens quietly all the time and answers when he hears his name. Say "BMO, what time is it" in one go, or just "BMO" and then talk. It keeps the microphone open, so it uses more battery.

Raise the pause if he cuts you off. In hold mode, keep your finger on the red button while you talk and let go when you're done — he can never interrupt.
